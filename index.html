Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to questions about booking procedures, security, shifts, and farmhouse policies.

How can I book Al Zameer Farm House?

You can easily reserve your date online using the "Book Now" button above. Choose your preferred shift, pick available dates, select optional add-ons, and submit. An instant booking slip will be created, and you can confirm it with our manager directly via WhatsApp or Phone.

What are the exact check-in and check-out timings for shifts?

- Day Shift: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Night Shift: 8:00 PM to 6:00 AM next morning
- 24 Hours Stay: Flexible check-in (e.g. 10:00 AM to 8:00 AM next day).

Is the standby generator fuel included in the package price?

Yes! Standby generator operation during power outages is provided so your party, air conditioning, and swimming pool pumps run smoothly without disruption.

Are families and corporate events both allowed?

Yes. Al Zameer is designed with 100% boundary wall privacy and armed security. We welcome decent families, corporate teams, and private gatherings. Unruly or stag behavior violating community decency is strictly prohibited.

How much advance token money is required to lock a date?

A 50% advance token via Bank Transfer, JazzCash, or Raast is required to lock your date on our calendar. The remaining balance is payable upon arrival at the farmhouse.
